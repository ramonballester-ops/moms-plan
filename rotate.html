Rotate the passcode

Decrypts the plan with the current passcode and re-encrypts it under a new one, with a fresh salt and IV. Everything happens on this device — neither passcode is ever sent anywhere.

Use four or more unrelated words. Case is ignored, so length is what protects it.

After it downloads:
  1. Open the moms-plan repo on GitHub
  2. Click data.enc.json → the pencil/upload icon → replace it with the file you just downloaded
  3. Commit. Pages redeploys in about a minute.
  4. Give your mom the new passcode — her device will ask for it once, then remember it.
